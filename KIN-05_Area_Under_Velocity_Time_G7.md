## Area Under the Curve

The **area** between the line on a **velocity-time** graph and the horizontal axis equals the **displacement**.

<center>

<img src="/v-t_area.excalidraw.svg" width="800" />

</center>

---

## Units of Area Under the Curve

$$ \text{area} = \text{velocity} \cdot \text{time} = \frac{\mathrm{m}}{\mathrm{s}} \cdot \mathrm{s} = \mathrm{m} $$

- The unit are **meter** which is the unit of **displacement**.

## Summary

- The **area** under a **velocity-time** graph is the **displacement**.
- Area **above** the axis is **positive** displacement; area **below** is **negative**.
- Units of area: $\frac{\mathrm{m}}{\mathrm{s}} \cdot \mathrm{s} = \mathrm{m}$.
