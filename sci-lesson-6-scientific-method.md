## The Scientific Method

<v-clicks>

- Observation: Watching and recording what happens in nature. This helps scientists spot patterns and ask questions.
- Question: Asking something specific you want to find out. A good question is clear and can be tested.
- Hypothesis: An educated guess that answers the question. It's a "if... then..." statement you can test.
- Experiment: A fair test where you change one thing at a time and measure what happens.
- Conclusion: Deciding if your hypothesis was right or wrong based on the data you collected.

</v-clicks>

---

## Scientific Method Flow

| Step        | What happens                                              |
| ----------- | --------------------------------------------------------- |
| Observation | Watch and record; spot patterns, ask questions            |
| Question    | Ask something specific that can be tested                 |
| Hypothesis  | Make an educated "if... then..." guess                    |
| Experiment  | Run a fair test; change only one thing at a time          |
| Analysis    | Study the data you collected                              |
| Conclusion  | Decide if the hypothesis was right or wrong               |

- An experiment is a controlled test: change only one variable at a time.

---

## Hypothesis

<img src="/hypothesis-writing.png" width="400" />

- A **hypothesis** is a testable and falsifiable guess as to why something happens.
- Written as an "if... then..." statement:
  - *If* the ramp is longer, *then* the ball will roll farther.

---

## Variables

<v-clicks>

- Independent Variable: The variable you change on purpose. Plotted on the x-axis.
- Dependent Variable: The variable you measure. Plotted on the y-axis.
- Control Variable: Variables kept constant.
- Control Group: The group that does not receive the treatment; used as a baseline for comparison.
- Experimental Group: The group that receives the treatment or independent variable.

</v-clicks>

---

## Example: Car Spoiler

- Hypothesis: adding a spoiler will reduce drag force.

| Variable | In this experiment                                   |
| -------- | ---------------------------------------------------- |
| Independent | The shape and size of the spoiler (what we change) |
| Dependent   | Drag force (what we measure)                        |
| Control     | Test cars without spoilers (baseline)               |

---

## After the Experiment

<v-clicks>

- Compare data against your hypothesis.
- Was the hypothesis supported or not supported?
- A conclusion states what the evidence showed — even if the hypothesis was wrong.

</v-clicks>
