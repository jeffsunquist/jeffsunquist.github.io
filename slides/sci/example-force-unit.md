## Example 3: Force

- The formula for **force** is:

$$F = m \times a$$

<v-click>

- $m$ has the unit $\text{kg}$
- $a$ has the unit $\frac{\text{m}}{\text{s}^2}$.

</v-click>
<v-click>

$$F = \text{kg}\cdot\frac{\text{m}}{\text{s}^2} = \text{N}$$
- *kilograms meters per second squared*, *Newton*

</v-click>
