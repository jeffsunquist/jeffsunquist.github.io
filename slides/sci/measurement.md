## What Is a Measurement?

>[!NOTE] Measurement
> An observation that compares an unknown quantity to a known quantity.

| Tool        | Quantity Measured      | SI Units                |
| :---------- | :--------------------- | :---------------------- |
| Meter stick | distance, displacement | meter, $\mathrm{m}$     |
| Stopwatch   | time                   | second, $\mathrm{s}$    |
| Mass scale  | mass                   | kilogram, $\mathrm{kg}$ |
| Force scale | force                  | Newton, $\mathrm{N}$    |
