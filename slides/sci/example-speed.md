## Example 1


A train travels <mark>$180 \text{ km}$</mark> in <mark>$2 \text{ h}$</mark>. What is its <mark>$\text{speed}$</mark>?


<v-click>

$s = \frac{d}{t} = \frac{180 \text{ km}}{2 \text{ h}} = 90 \frac{\text{km}}{\text{h}}$

</v-click>

<v-click>

$s$: $\frac{\text{km}}{\text{h}}$, scalar

</v-click>
