## Scalars and Vectors

> [!NOTE] Scalar
> A quantity with a magnitude only (e.g. **speed** of $90 \frac{\text{km}}{\text{h}}$).

> [!NOTE] Vector
> A quantity magnitude _and direction_ (e.g. **velocity** of $90 \frac{\text{km}}{\text{h}}$ _east_).
