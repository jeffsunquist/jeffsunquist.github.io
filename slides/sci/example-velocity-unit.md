## Example 1: Velocity

- The formula for **average velocity** is:

$$\bar v = \frac{\Delta x}{t}$$

<v-click>

- $\Delta x$, displacement, has the unit **meters ($\text{m}$)**
- $t$, time, has the unit **seconds ($\text{s}$)** .

</v-click>
<v-click>

$$\bar v = \frac{\text{m}}{\text{s}}$$
- *meters per second*

</v-click>
