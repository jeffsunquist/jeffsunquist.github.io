## Review: Graphing

- **Tables** organize data; the independent variable goes in the first column.
- **Line graph** — continuous data; **bar graph** — categories and comparisons; **pie chart** — parts of a whole.
- **SULTAN** — **S**cale, **U**nits, **L**egend, **T**itle, **A**xes, **N**eat.
- The **line of best fit** follows the trend of the points (don't connect them one by one).
