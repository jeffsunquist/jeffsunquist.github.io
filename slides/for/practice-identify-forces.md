## Practice: Identify the Forces

A crate is pulled to the right by a rope across a rough floor.

<div class="absolute bottom-16 left-0 right-0 pr-8 text-right">

<v-clicks>

**Answer:** tension **right**; friction **left**; normal **up**; weight **down**. Causes: rope, floor, gravity.

</v-clicks>
