## Example: Lifting a Bucket

A rope pulls a bucket up with $\overset{F_T}{\underline{50\ \mathrm{N}}}$; gravity pulls down with $\overset{F_g}{\underline{80\ \mathrm{N}}}$. Calculate the $\overset{F_{net}}{\underline{\text{net force}}}$.

<v-click>

<center>

</center>

</v-click>

<v-click>

$F_{net} = F_T + F_g = 50\ \mathrm{N} - 80\ \mathrm{N} = \boxed{-30\ \mathrm{N}}$ or $\boxed{30\ \mathrm{N\ down}}$

</v-click>
