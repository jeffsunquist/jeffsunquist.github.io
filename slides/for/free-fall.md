## Free Fall

> [!NOTE]
> **Free fall** is motion under gravity alone, with air resistance neglected.

((free_fall_feather_hammer: a feather and a hammer falling together in a vacuum chamber and landing at the same time))

- In free fall, every object accelerates at $a = g = 9.8\ \mathrm{m/s^2}$, regardless of its mass.
- Air resistance is what makes a feather fall more slowly than a hammer in a room.
