## Free Fall

> [!NOTE] Free Fall
> The motion under gravity alone, with zero (or negligible) air resistance.

- In free fall, every object accelerates at $a = g = 9.8\ \mathrm{\frac{m}{s^2}}$, regardless of its mass.
- In free fall, a feather and a bowling ball will fall at the same rate.
