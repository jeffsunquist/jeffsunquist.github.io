## Example: Hanging a Ceiling Lamp

A lamp hangs from a ceiling. The chain pulls up with $\overset{F_T}{\underline{60\ \mathrm{N}}}$ while gravity pulls down with $\overset{F_g}{\underline{60\ \mathrm{N}}}$. Calculate the $\overset{F_{net}}{\underline{\text{net force}}}$.

<v-click>

<center>

<img src="/FBD_lamp_hanging_from_ceiling.svg" width="70">

</center>

</v-click>

<v-click>

$F_{net} = F_T + F_g = 60\ \mathrm{N} - 60\ \mathrm{N} = \boxed{0\ \mathrm{N}}$

</v-click>
