## Newton's First Law

> [!IMPORTANT]
> An object's velocity will not change unless a **net force** acts on it.

<center>

<img src="/frictionless_hockey_puck.excalidraw.svg" width="540">

</center>

- An object at rest stays at rest.
- A moving object keeps a **constant velocity** — same speed, same direction — unless a net force acts.
