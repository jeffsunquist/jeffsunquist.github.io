## Example: Force, Acceleration, and Velocity

The cart is already moving right at $2\ \mathrm{\frac{m}{s}}$. A push of $20\ \mathrm{N}$ acts right while friction is $8\ \mathrm{N}$ left. This lasts for $5\ \mathrm{s}$.

Find its acceleration and final velocity.

$$F_{net} = 20\ \mathrm{N} - 8\ \mathrm{N} = 12\ \mathrm{N\,right}$$

$$a = \frac{F_{net}}{m} = \frac{12\ \mathrm{N}}{4\ \mathrm{kg}} = 3\ \mathrm{\frac{m}{s^2}\,right}$$

$$v = v_0 + at = 2\ \mathrm{\frac{m}{s}} + \left(3\ \mathrm{\frac{m}{s^2}}\right)(5\ \mathrm{s}) = \boxed{17\ \mathrm{\frac{m}{s}\,right}}$$
