## Review: Mass, Inertia, and Newton's First Law

- **Mass** is the amount of matter in an object, measured in **kilograms ($\mathrm{kg}$)**.
- Taking an object to another world does not change its mass.
- **Inertia** is an object's tendency to resist a change in motion; **more mass means more inertia**.
- **Newton's first law** (the **law of inertia**): an object keeps its motion unless a **net force** acts on it.
