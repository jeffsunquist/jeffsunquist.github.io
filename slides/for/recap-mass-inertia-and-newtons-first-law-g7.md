## Review: Mass, Inertia, and Newton's First Law

- **Mass** is the amount of matter in an object, measured in **kilograms (kg)**; it is a scalar and is the same everywhere.
- **Inertia** is an object's tendency to resist a change in motion; **more mass means more inertia**.
- Compare two objects' inertia by comparing their **masses**; more inertia means a **smaller acceleration** for the same net force.
- **Newton's first law** (the **law of inertia**): an object keeps its motion unless a **net force** acts on it.
