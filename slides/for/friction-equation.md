## The Friction Equation

> [!IMPORTANT]
> $f = \mu N$
> $f$ = friction force (N); $\mu$ = coefficient of friction (no units); $N$ = normal force (N)

- A larger $\mu$ or a larger $N$ gives a larger friction force.
- On a flat surface with only weight pressing down, $N = mg$.

| Surface pair | $\mu$ (rough guide) |
| --- | --- |
| rubber on dry concrete | $0.8$ |
| wood on wood | $0.4$ |
| steel on ice | $0.05$ |
