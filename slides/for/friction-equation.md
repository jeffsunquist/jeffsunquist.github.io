## The Friction Equation

> [!IMPORTANT]
> $F_f = \mu F_N$ is the **template** for friction. Fill it in for the situation:
>
> **Moving:** $F_k = \mu_k F_N$
>
> **At rest:** $F_s \le \mu_s F_N$, with maximum $F_{s,\max} = \mu_s F_N$

Static friction will oppose an applied force, until the applied force is too strong. Then, the object will begin to move and kinetic friction will replace static friction.
