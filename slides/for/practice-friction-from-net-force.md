## Practice: Kinetic Friction from Net Force

A box slides right while pushed with $55\ \mathrm{N}$. The net force is $20\ \mathrm{N}$ right. Find the kinetic friction force.

<div class="absolute bottom-16 left-0 right-0 pr-8 text-right">

<v-click>

**Answer:** $\boxed{F_k = 35\ \mathrm{N\,left}}$
