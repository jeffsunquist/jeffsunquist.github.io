## Static Friction vs Kinetic Friction

- Static friction is stronger than kinetic friction. It is **harder to start** an object moving than to **keep it moving**.
- The **coefficient of static friction** is larger than the coefficient of kinetic friction for the same surfaces: $\mu_s > \mu_k$

> [!NOTE] coefficient of friction
> A factor of how grippy two surfaces are. It has **no units**: $\mu$ (generic), $\mu_s$ (static), $\mu_k$ (kinetic).
