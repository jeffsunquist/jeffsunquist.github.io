## Static Friction Is Stronger Than Kinetic Friction

- It is **harder to start** an object moving than to **keep it moving**.
- The **coefficient of static friction** is larger than the coefficient of kinetic friction for the same surfaces:
$\mu_s > \mu_k$

- So the **maximum static friction** is larger than the kinetic friction:
$F_{s,\max} > F_k$

- Static friction only grows as large as it needs to — up to that maximum.
