## Example: Unknown Tension

A loaded elevator has a weight of $\overset{F_g}{\underline{12\,000\ \mathrm{N}}}$ downward. The $\overset{F_{net}}{\underline{\text{net force}}}$ on it is $\overset{F_{net}}{\underline{2\,000\ \mathrm{N}}}$ upward. Calculate the $\overset{F_T}{\underline{\text{tension}}}$ in the cable.

<center>

<img src="/FBD_elevator.excalidraw.svg" width="70">

</center>

<v-clicks>

$F_{net} = F_T + F_g$

$F_T = (F_{net}) - (F_g) = (2\,000\ \mathrm{N}) - (-12\,000\ \mathrm{N}) = \boxed{14\,000\ \mathrm{N}\ \text{up}}$

</v-clicks>
