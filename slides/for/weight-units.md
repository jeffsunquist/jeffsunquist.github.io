## The Unit of Weight

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Because weight is a force, its SI unit is the **newton (N)**.

| Quantity | Unit | Symbol |
| --- | --- | --- |
| weight | newton | $\mathrm{N}$ |
| mass | kilogram | $\mathrm{kg}$ |

- A $1\ \mathrm{kg}$ mass weighs about $9.8\ \mathrm{N}$ on Earth.
