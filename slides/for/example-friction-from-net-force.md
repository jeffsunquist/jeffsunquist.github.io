## Example: Kinetic Friction from Net Force

A box slides right while pushed with $\overset{F_A}{\underline{40\ \mathrm{N}}}$. The $\overset{F_{net}}{\underline{\text{net force}}}$ is $\overset{F_{net}}{\underline{25\ \mathrm{N}}}$ right. Find the $\overset{F_k}{\underline{\text{kinetic friction force}}}$.

<v-clicks>

<center>

<img src="/FBD_push_box_with_friction.svg" width="300">

</center>



$F_{net} = F_A - F_k$

$F_k = F_A - F_{net}$

$F_k = (40\ \mathrm{N}) - (25\ \mathrm{N}) = \boxed{15\ \mathrm{N\,left}}$

</v-clicks>
