## Example: Identify the Forces

A book rests on a table.

<v-click>

- $\overset{F_g}{\underline{\text{weight}}}$: non-contact; Earth pulls the book **down**.

</v-click>

<v-click>

- $\overset{F_N}{\underline{\text{normal force}}}$: contact; the table pushes the book **up**.

</v-click>
