## Example: From Force to Motion

A $5\ \mathrm{kg}$ box is pushed from rest with a net force of $20\ \mathrm{N}$ for $3\ \mathrm{s}$. Find its acceleration and its final velocity.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> First use $F_{net} = ma$, then the kinematic equations:
> $v = v_0 + at \qquad \Delta x = v_0 t + \tfrac{1}{2}at^2$

<v-clicks>

**Solution**

A <mark>$5\ \mathrm{kg}$</mark> box starts <mark>from rest</mark> under a <mark>$20\ \mathrm{N}$</mark> net force for <mark>$3\ \mathrm{s}$</mark>.

$a = \dfrac{20\ \mathrm{N}}{5\ \mathrm{kg}} = 4\ \mathrm{m/s^2}$

$v = v_0 + at = 0 + (4\ \mathrm{m/s^2})(3\ \mathrm{s}) = 12\ \mathrm{m/s}$

$v$: $\mathrm{m/s}$, in the direction of the net force

</v-clicks>
