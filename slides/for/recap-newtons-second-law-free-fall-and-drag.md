## Review: Newton's Second Law, Free Fall, and Drag

- **Newton's second law**: a net force causes an acceleration in its direction, $F_{net} = ma$.
- For the same force, more mass gives **less** acceleration.
- **Free fall** is motion under gravity alone (no air resistance): every object accelerates at $g = 9.8\ \text{m/s}^2$, regardless of mass.
- **Drag** opposes motion through air and grows with speed; at **terminal velocity** drag equals weight, so the net force is zero and the velocity is constant.
