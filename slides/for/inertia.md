## Inertia

> [!NOTE]
> **Inertia** is the tendency of an object to resist a change in its motion.

((inertia_bus_lurch: a standing passenger on a bus that brakes, with the passenger continuing forward))

- Inertia is why moving objects keep moving and still objects stay still.
- **More mass means more inertia.**
