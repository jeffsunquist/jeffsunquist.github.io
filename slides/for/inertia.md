## Inertia

> [!NOTE] Inertia
> The tendency of an object to resist a change in its motion.

((inertia_bus_lurch: a standing passenger on a bus that brakes, with the passenger continuing forward))

- Inertia is why moving objects keep moving and objects at rest stay at rest.
- **More mass means more inertia.**
