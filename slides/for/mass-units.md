## The Kilogram (kg)

> [!IMPORTANT]
> The SI unit of mass is the **kilogram (kg)**.

| Quantity | Unit | Symbol |
| --- | --- | --- |
| mass | kilogram | $\mathrm{kg}$ |
| gram | $1\ \mathrm{g} = 0.001\ \mathrm{kg}$ | $\mathrm{g}$ |

- A $2\ \mathrm{kg}$ bag of rice has a mass of $2\ \mathrm{kg}$ on Earth **and** on the Moon.
