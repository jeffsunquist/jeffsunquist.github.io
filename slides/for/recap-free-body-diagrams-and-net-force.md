## Review: Free-Body Diagrams and Net Force

- A **force** is a push or a pull — a **vector** with magnitude and direction.
- A **free-body diagram** shows every force acting on **one object** as labeled arrows drawn from a dot.
- The **net force** combines all the forces: $F_{net} = \sum F$ (add same direction, subtract opposite).
- The SI unit of force is the **newton (N)**: $1\ \text{N} = 1\ \text{kg}\cdot\text{m/s}^2$.
- **Balanced** forces give a net force of zero; **unbalanced** forces give a nonzero net force.
