## Review: Free-Body Diagrams and Net Force

- A **force** is a push or a pull. It is a **vector**.
- The SI unit of force is the **newton (N)**
  - $1\ \text{N} = 1\ \mathrm{kg\cdot m/s^2}$.
- A **free-body diagram** shows every force acting on **one object** as labeled arrows drawn from a dot.
- The **net force** is the vector sum of the forces.
- **Balanced** forces give $F_{net} = 0$; **unbalanced** forces give a nonzero net force and change the motion.
