## Mass

> [!NOTE]
> **Mass** is the amount of matter in an object. It is measured in **kilograms (kg)**.

((mass_kg_balance: a balance with a 1 kg standard mass on one side))

- Mass is a **scalar** — it has magnitude but no direction.
- Mass is the same everywhere; it does not change with location.
