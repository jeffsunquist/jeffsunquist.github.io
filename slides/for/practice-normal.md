## Practice: Normal Force

A $12\ \mathrm{kg}$ box rests on a table. Find the normal force on the box.

<div class="absolute bottom-16 left-0 right-0 pr-8 text-right">

<v-clicks>

**Answer:** $F_N = F_g = mg = (12\ \mathrm{kg})(9.8\ \mathrm{\frac{m}{s^2}}) = \boxed{117.6\ \mathrm{N\,up}}$

</v-clicks>
