## Review: Universal Gravitation

- **Newton's law of universal gravitation**: $F = G\dfrac{m_1 m_2}{r^2}$, with $G = 6.67 \times 10^{-11}\ \text{N}\cdot\text{m}^2/\text{kg}^2$.
- Every mass attracts every other mass.
- The force increases with the **masses** and decreases with the **square of the distance**.
- **Weight** is the gravitational force between an object and a planet.
