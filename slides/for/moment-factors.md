## What Changes a Moment

- Increasing the **force** increases the moment.
- Increasing the **perpendicular distance** from the pivot increases the moment.

((moment_factors: two panels — the same force at a longer arm, and a larger force at the same arm, both giving a bigger moment))

| Change | Effect on the moment |
| --- | --- |
| force doubled | moment doubled |
| distance doubled | moment doubled |
| force applied **at** the pivot | no moment at all |
