## The Principle of Moments

> [!IMPORTANT]
> A beam balances when the total clockwise moment equals the total anticlockwise moment:
> $\sum M_{clockwise} = \sum M_{anticlockwise}$

((seesaw_balance: a seesaw with a heavier child close to the pivot and a lighter child far from it, moments labelled))

- Use the **perpendicular** distance from the pivot for each force.
- The two moments must be equal in size and opposite in direction of turning.
