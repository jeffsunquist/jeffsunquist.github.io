## Newton's Law of Universal Gravitation

> [!IMPORTANT]
> $F = G\dfrac{m_1 m_2}{r^2}$
> $F$ = gravitational force (N); $m_1, m_2$ = masses (kg); $r$ = distance between centers (m)
> $G = 6.67 \times 10^{-11}\ \text{N}\cdot\text{m}^2/\text{kg}^2$

- Every mass attracts every other mass.
- The force increases with the masses and decreases with the square of the distance.
- Weight is the gravitational force between an object and a planet.
