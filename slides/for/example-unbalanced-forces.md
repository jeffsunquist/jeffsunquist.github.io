## Example: Unbalanced Forces

Two teams pull a rope in opposite directions with $\overset{F_A}{\underline{600\ \mathrm{N}}}\,\mathrm{left}$ and $\overset{F_B}{\underline{400\ \mathrm{N}}}\,\mathrm{right}$. Calculate the $\overset{F_{net}}{\underline{\text{net force}}}$ and state whether the forces are balanced.

<v-click>

<center>

<img src="/FBD_two_people_pulling_unbalanced.excalidraw.svg" width="360">

</center>

</v-click>

<v-click>

$F_{net} = F_A + F_B = -600\ \mathrm{N} + 400\ \mathrm{N} = \boxed{-200\ \mathrm{N}}$ or $\boxed{200\,\mathrm{N\,left}}$

</v-click>

<v-click>

The net force is not zero. The forces are **unbalanced**, so the rope accelerates to the left.

</v-click>
