## Example: Gravitational Force

Two masses, $5\ \mathrm{kg}$ and $3\ \mathrm{kg}$, are $2\ \mathrm{m}$ apart. Find the gravitational force between them.

<v-clicks>

**Solution**

Masses <mark>$5\ \mathrm{kg}$</mark> and <mark>$3\ \mathrm{kg}$</mark>, separated by <mark>$2\ \mathrm{m}$</mark>. Find the <mark>gravitational force</mark>.

$F = G\dfrac{m_1 m_2}{r^2} = (6.67\times10^{-11})\dfrac{(5)(3)}{2^2}$

$F = 2.5 \times 10^{-10}\ \mathrm{N}$

$F$: $\mathrm{N}$, attractive (toward each other)

</v-clicks>
