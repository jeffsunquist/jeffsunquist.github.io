## Review: Moments

- The **moment (turning effect)** of a force is its turning effect about a **pivot**.
- $M = Fd$, where $d$ is the **perpendicular** distance from the pivot; the unit is the **newton-metre (N·m)**.
- Increasing the **force** or the **perpendicular distance** increases the moment.
- **Principle of moments**: a beam balances when $\sum M_{clockwise} = \sum M_{anticlockwise}$.
