## Review: Forces and Equilibrium

- **Equilibrium** is the state when the net force on an object is **zero**.
- Balanced forces $\Leftrightarrow$ equilibrium $\Leftrightarrow$ zero net force $\Leftrightarrow$ zero acceleration.
- In equilibrium the object moves at a **constant velocity** (which may be zero).
- **Static equilibrium**: at rest; **dynamic equilibrium**: moving at constant velocity.
- To solve force-and-motion problems: use $F_{net} = ma$ to find the acceleration, then the kinematic equations to describe the motion.
