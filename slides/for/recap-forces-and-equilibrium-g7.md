## Review: Forces and Equilibrium

- **Equilibrium** is the state when the net force on an object is **zero**.
- Balanced forces $\Leftrightarrow$ equilibrium $\Leftrightarrow$ zero net force $\Leftrightarrow$ zero acceleration $\Leftrightarrow$ constant velocity.
- **Static equilibrium**: at rest; **dynamic equilibrium**: moving at constant velocity.
- To solve a force-and-motion problem: use $F_{net}=ma$ to find the acceleration, then the G7 kinematics equations to find velocity and displacement.
