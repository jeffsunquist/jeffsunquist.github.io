## Example: From Net Force to Acceleration

A $\overset{m}{\underline{5\ \mathrm{kg}}}$ box is pushed right with $\overset{F_{push}}{\underline{30\ \mathrm{N}}}$ while friction pulls left with $\overset{F_f}{\underline{10\ \mathrm{N}}}$.

Find its net force and acceleration.

<v-clicks>

$$F_{net} = F_{push} + F_f = 30\ \mathrm{N} - 10\ \mathrm{N} = 20\ \mathrm{N\,right}$$

$$a = \frac{F_{net}}{m} = \frac{20\ \mathrm{N}}{5\ \mathrm{kg}} = \boxed{4\ \mathrm{\frac{m}{s^2}\,right}}$$

</v-clicks>
