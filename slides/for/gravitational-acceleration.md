## Gravitational Acceleration

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Gravitational acceleration** (also called *acceleration due to gravity*, or simply *gravity*) is the acceleration an object has because of gravity.
> On Earth: $g = 9.8\ \text{m/s}^2$

- $g$ points downward, toward the center of the planet.
