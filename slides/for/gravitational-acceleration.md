## Gravitational Acceleration

> [!NOTE] Gravitational Acceleration
> Aalso called *acceleration due to gravity*, or simply *gravity*, is the acceleration an object has because of gravity. Its units are $\mathrm{\frac{m}{s^2}}$

((g_earth_9_8: the Earth with a downward arrow labelled g = 9.8 m/s²))

- $g$ points **downward**, toward the centre of the planet.
- Every falling object near Earth accelerates at $9.8\ \mathrm{m/s^2}$, whatever its mass.
