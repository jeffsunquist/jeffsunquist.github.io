## Gravitational Acceleration

> [!NOTE] Gravitational Acceleration
> Also called *acceleration due to gravity*, or simply $\mathrm{g}$, is the acceleration an object has because of gravity. Its units are $\mathrm{\frac{m}{s^2}}$
