## Net Force Determines Acceleration

- The net force is both the **sum of the forces** and the **determining factor for acceleration**.

((net_force_acceleration: three panels — F_net right with a rightward acceleration, F_net left with a leftward acceleration, F_net = 0 with no acceleration))

- Zero net force gives **zero** acceleration.
- A nonzero net force gives acceleration **in the direction of the net force**.
