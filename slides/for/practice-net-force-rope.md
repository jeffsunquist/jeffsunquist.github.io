## Example: Balanced Forces

Two teams pull a rope with $\overset{F_A}{\underline{500\ \mathrm{N}}}$ and $\overset{F_B}{\underline{500\ \mathrm{N}}}$, in opposite directions. Calculate the $\overset{F_{net}}{\underline{\text{net force}}}$ and state whether the forces are balanced.

<v-click>

<center>

<img src="/FBD_two_people_pulling_opposite.excalidraw.svg" width="320">

</center>

</v-click>

<v-click>

$F_{net} = F_A + F_B = 500\ \mathrm{N} - 500\ \mathrm{N} = \boxed{0\ \mathrm{N}}$

</v-click>

<v-click>

The net force is zero. The forces are **balanced**.

</v-click>
