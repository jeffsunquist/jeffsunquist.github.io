## Air Resistance and Terminal Velocity

- **Air resistance** opposes motion and **increases** as speed increases.

((terminal_velocity_stages: three stages of a falling skydiver — small drag and large net force, growing drag, then drag equal to weight))

> [!TIP] 
> At **terminal velocity** the air resistance equals the weight, so the net force is zero and the velocity is constant.
