## Drag and Terminal Velocity

- **Air resistance (drag)** opposes motion through air and **increases** as speed increases.

((terminal_velocity_stages: three stages of a falling skydiver — small drag and large net force, growing drag, then drag equal to weight))

| Stage | Drag vs weight | Net force | Motion |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| just jumped | drag $<$ weight | downward | speeding up |
| later | drag $=$ weight | zero | **terminal velocity** |

> [!TIP]
> At **terminal velocity** the drag equals the weight, so the net force is zero and the velocity is constant.
