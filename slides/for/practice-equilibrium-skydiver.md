## Practice: A Skydiver

A skydiver jumps from a plane.

<center>

((skydiver_two_states: the skydiver just after jumping, with air resistance less than weight, beside the skydiver at terminal velocity, with air resistance equal to weight))

</center>

<v-clicks>

**Before terminal velocity:** $F_{ar} < F_g$, so the net force is downward. **Not** in equilibrium; speeding up.

**At terminal velocity:** $F_{ar} = F_g$, so the net force is zero. **In** equilibrium; constant velocity.

</v-clicks>
