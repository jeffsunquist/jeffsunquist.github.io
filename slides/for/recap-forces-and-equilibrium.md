## Review: Forces and Equilibrium

- **Equilibrium** is the state when the net force on an object is **zero**.
- In equilibrium the acceleration is zero, so the object moves at a **constant velocity** (which may be zero — at rest).
- To solve a force-and-motion problem: use $F_{net}=ma$ to find the acceleration, then $a=\Delta v/t$ to find the change in velocity.
