## The Newton (N)

> [!IMPORTANT]
> The SI unit of force is the **newton (N)**.
> $1\ \mathrm{N} = 1\ \mathrm{kg}\cdot\mathrm{m/s^2}$

<center>

<img src="/FBD_one_newton.svg" width="280">

</center>

- A newton is the force needed to give a $1\ \mathrm{kg}$ mass an acceleration of $1\ \mathrm{m/s^2}$.
