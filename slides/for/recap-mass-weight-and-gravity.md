## Review: Mass, Weight, and Gravity

- **Mass** is a scalar measured in **kilograms (kg)**; it is the same everywhere.
- **Weight** is the force of gravity on an object, measured in **newtons (N)**, and it always acts **downward**.
- $W = mg$, where $g$ is the local gravitational acceleration (on Earth, $g = 9.8\ \text{m/s}^2$).
- On other worlds $g$ is different, so the same mass has a different **weight**.
