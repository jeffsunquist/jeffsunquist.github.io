## Example: A Moment

A $20\ \mathrm{N}$ force is applied $0.5\ \mathrm{m}$ from the pivot of a spanner. Find the moment.

<v-clicks>

**Solution**

A <mark>$20\ \mathrm{N}$</mark> force is applied <mark>$0.5\ \mathrm{m}$</mark> from the <mark>pivot</mark>. Find the <mark>moment</mark>.

$M = Fd = 20\ \mathrm{N} \cdot 0.5\ \mathrm{m} = 10\ \mathrm{N\cdot m}$

$M$: $\mathrm{N\cdot m}$; clockwise or anticlockwise must be stated

</v-clicks>
