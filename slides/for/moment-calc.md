## Calculating a Moment

> [!IMPORTANT]
> $M = Fd$
> $M$ = moment (N·m); $F$ = force (N); $d$ = perpendicular distance from the pivot (m)

## Example

A $20\ \text{N}$ force applied $0.5\ \text{m}$ from the pivot:

<v-clicks>

- $M = Fd = 20\ \text{N} \cdot 0.5\ \text{m} = 10\ \text{N·m}$

</v-clicks>
