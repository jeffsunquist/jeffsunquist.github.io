## Practice — $F = ma$

A $20\ \mathrm{N}$ net force gives a box an acceleration of $4\ \mathrm{m/s^2}$.

<div class="absolute bottom-8 left-0 right-0 pr-8 text-right">

<v-click>

**Answer:** $m = \dfrac{F_{net}}{a} = \dfrac{20\ \mathrm{N}}{4\ \mathrm{m/s^2}} = 5\ \mathrm{kg}$

</v-click>

</div>
