## Example: Unknown Friction

A box is pushed right with $\overset{F_A}{\underline{30\ \mathrm{N}}}$. The $\overset{F_{net}}{\underline{\text{net force}}}$ is $\overset{F_{net}}{\underline{25\ \mathrm{N}}}$ right. Calculate the $\overset{F_f}{\underline{\text{friction force}}}$.

<center>

<img src="/FBD_push_box_with_friction.svg" width="300">

</center>

<v-clicks>

$F_{net} = F_A + F_f$

$F_f = F_{net} - F_A$

$F_f = (25\ \mathrm{N}) - (30\ \mathrm{N}) = \boxed{-5\ \mathrm{N}}$ or $\boxed{5\,\mathrm{N\,left}}$

</v-clicks>
