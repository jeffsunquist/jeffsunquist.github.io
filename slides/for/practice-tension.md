## Practice: Tension in a Cord

A $5\ \mathrm{kg}$ sign hangs motionless from one cord. Find the tension in the cord.

<div class="absolute bottom-16 left-0 right-0 pr-8 text-right">

<v-clicks>

**Answer:** $F_T = F_g = mg = (5\ \mathrm{kg})(9.8\ \mathrm{\frac{m}{s^2}}) = \boxed{49\ \mathrm{N\,up}}$

</v-clicks>
