## Example: Pushing a Box

Two students push a box to the right with $\overset{F_A}{\underline{40\ \mathrm{N}}}$ and $\overset{F_B}{\underline{25\ \mathrm{N}}}$. Friction pushes left with $\overset{F_f}{\underline{15\ \mathrm{N}}}$. Calculate the $\overset{F_{net}}{\underline{\text{net force}}}$.

<v-click>

<center>

<img src="/net_force_pushing_box.svg" width="360">

</center>

</v-click>

<v-click>

$F_{net} = F_A + F_B + F_f = 40\ \mathrm{N} + 25\ \mathrm{N} - 15\ \mathrm{N} = \boxed{50\ \mathrm{N}\,\mathrm{right}}$

</v-click>
