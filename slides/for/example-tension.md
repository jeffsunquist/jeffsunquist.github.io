## Example: Tension in a Cord

A $3\ \mathrm{kg}$ lamp hangs motionless from one cord. Find the tension in the cord.

<v-clicks>

**Solution**

A <mark>$3\ \mathrm{kg}$</mark> lamp hangs <mark>motionless</mark> from <mark>one cord</mark>. Find the <mark>tension</mark>.

$T = W = mg = 3\ \mathrm{kg} \cdot 9.8\ \mathrm{m/s^2} = 29.4\ \mathrm{N}$

$T$: $\mathrm{N}$, upward

</v-clicks>
