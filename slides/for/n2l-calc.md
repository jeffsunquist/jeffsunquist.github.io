## Using F = ma

> [!IMPORTANT]
> $F_{net} = ma \quad\Rightarrow\quad a = \dfrac{F_{net}}{m}$

## Example

A $2\ \text{kg}$ cart is pushed with a net force of $10\ \text{N}$:

<v-clicks>

- $a = \dfrac{F_{net}}{m} = \dfrac{10\ \text{N}}{2\ \text{kg}} = 5\ \text{m/s}^2$

</v-clicks>
