## Example: Balancing a Seesaw

A $40\ \mathrm{kg}$ child sits $1.5\ \mathrm{m}$ from the pivot. How far from the pivot must a $20\ \mathrm{kg}$ child sit to balance the beam?

<v-clicks>

**Solution**

A <mark>$40\ \mathrm{kg}$</mark> child sits <mark>$1.5\ \mathrm{m}$</mark> from the pivot. Find the <mark>distance</mark> for a <mark>$20\ \mathrm{kg}$</mark> child.

$W_1 d_1 = W_2 d_2 \;\Rightarrow\; (40)(1.5) = (20)(d_2)$

$d_2 = \dfrac{60}{20} = 3\ \mathrm{m}$

$d_2$: $\mathrm{m}$, on the other side of the pivot

</v-clicks>
