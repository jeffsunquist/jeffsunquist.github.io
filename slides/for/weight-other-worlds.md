## Weight on Other Worlds

- On other worlds, $g$ is different, so the **weight** of the same object is different.

| World | $g\ (\mathrm{\frac{m}{s^2}})$ | Weight of $1\ \mathrm{kg}$ |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Earth | $9.8$ | $9.8\ \mathrm{N}$ |
| Moon | $1.6$ | $1.6\ \mathrm{N}$ |
| Mars | $3.7$ | $3.7\ \mathrm{N}$ |

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Use $F_g = mg$ with **that world's** value of $g$.
