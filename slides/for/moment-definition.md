## Moments

> [!NOTE]
> The **moment (turning effect)** of a force is its turning effect about a **pivot**.

((moment_lever_arm: a spanner turning a bolt, with the force arrow, the pivot, and the perpendicular distance d marked))

- A larger force, or a larger distance from the pivot, gives a larger moment.
- A moment is measured in **newton-metres (N·m)**.
