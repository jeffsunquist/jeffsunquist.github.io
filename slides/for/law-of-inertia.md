## The Law of Inertia

- Newton's first law is also called the **law of inertia**.
- It explains why motion continues unchanged until a net force acts.

| Everyday situation | Inertia explains it |
| --- | --- |
| a book stays on a desk | it is at rest and no net force acts |
| a puck glides across ice | almost no friction, so velocity stays constant |
| a passenger needs a seatbelt | their body keeps moving when the car stops |
