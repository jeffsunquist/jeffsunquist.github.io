## Comparing Inertia

- Compare the inertia of two objects by comparing their **masses**.
- More inertia means a **smaller acceleration** for the same net force.

((inertia_cart_vs_truck: an empty cart and a loaded truck given the same push, with the cart speeding up much more))

| Object | Mass | Inertia | Acceleration from the same push |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| empty cart | small | small | large |
| loaded truck | large | large | small |

> [!TIP]
> A $10\ \mathrm{kg}$ ball has ten times the inertia of a $1\ \mathrm{kg}$ ball — the same kick changes its motion far less.
