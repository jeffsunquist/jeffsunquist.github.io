## Practice: Using $F_{s,\max} = \mu_s F_N$

A crate sits on a floor with $F_N = 50\ \mathrm{N}$ and $\mu_s = 0.3$. Find the maximum static friction force.

<v-click>

<div class="absolute bottom-16 left-12 right-12 pr-8 text-right">

**Answer:** $\boxed{F_{s,\max} = 15\ \mathrm{N}}$.

</div>
