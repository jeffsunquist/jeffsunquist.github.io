## Solving for an Unknown Force

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Give every force its **sign**, and substitute each force **in parentheses** so the sign stays attached.

Rearrange $F_{net} = F_{known} + F_{unknown}$ and put parenthesis around each force to be plugged in:

$$F_{unknown} = (F_{net}) - (F_{known})$$



> [!TIP]
> The parentheses stop you from dropping a sign. For example, subtracting a negative force **adds** it:  $-\,(-5\ \mathrm{N})=+5\ \mathrm{N}$.
