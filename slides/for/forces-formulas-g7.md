## Forces Formulas

- $a = \frac{F_{net}}{m}$

- $|F_g| = mg \qquad \qquad \qquad g = 9.8\ \frac{\mathrm{m}}{\mathrm{s^2}}$

- $|F_f| = \mu |F_N|$

- $|F_G| = G\,\frac{m_1 m_2}{r^2} \qquad \qquad G = 6.67 \times 10^{-11}\ \mathrm{N\cdot m^2/kg^2}$

- $\tau = F \times d$

- $F_1 d_1 = F_2 d_2$
