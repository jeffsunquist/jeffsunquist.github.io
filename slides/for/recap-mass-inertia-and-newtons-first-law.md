## Review: Mass, Inertia, and Newton's First Law

- **Mass** is the amount of matter in an object, measured in **kilograms ($\mathrm{kg}$)**; it is a scalar and is the same everywhere.
- **Inertia** is an object's tendency to resist a change in motion; **more mass means more inertia**.
- **Newton's first law**: an object keeps its motion unless a **net force** acts on it.
- Balanced forces leave the motion unchanged; unbalanced forces change it.
