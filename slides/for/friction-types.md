## Two Types of Friction

**Static friction** $F_s$ acts on an object that is **not moving**. 

**Kinetic friction** $F_k$ acts on an object that is **sliding**. The general friction symbol is $F_f$.

<div style="height:120px; width:100%; border:1px dashed #999; background:#f5f5f5;"></div>

> [!TIP]
> The **coefficient of friction** describes how grippy two surfaces are. It has **no units**: $\mu$ (generic), $\mu_s$ (static), $\mu_k$ (kinetic).
