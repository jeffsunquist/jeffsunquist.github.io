## Review: Friction and the Friction Equation

- **Friction** opposes motion between surfaces that touch.
- **Kinetic friction** acts **opposite** to the direction of sliding; **static friction** holds a stationary object in place.
- $f = \mu N$, where $\mu$ is the coefficient of friction (no units) and $N$ is the normal force.
- A larger $\mu$ or a larger $N$ gives a larger friction force.
