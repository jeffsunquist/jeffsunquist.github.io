## Calculating Net Force

> [!IMPORTANT]
> $F_{net} = \sum F$

- Add forces acting in the **same** direction; subtract forces acting in **opposite** directions.

## Example

Two students push a box to the right with $40\ \text{N}$ and $25\ \text{N}$. Friction pushes left with $15\ \text{N}$.

<v-clicks>

- $F_{net} = 40\ \text{N} + 25\ \text{N} - 15\ \text{N} = 50\ \text{N}$ to the right

</v-clicks>
