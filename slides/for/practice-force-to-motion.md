## Practice: From Net Force to Motion

An $\overset{m}{\underline{8\ \mathrm{kg}}}$ cart is pushed to the right with $\overset{F_{push}}{\underline{40\ \mathrm{N}}}$ while friction pulls left with $\overset{F_f}{\underline{16\ \mathrm{N}}}$. Starting from rest, the cart moves for $\overset{t}{\underline{4\ \mathrm{s}}}$.

Find its acceleration and final velocity.

<div class="absolute bottom-8 left-0 right-0 pr-8 text-right">

<v-clicks>

**Answer:** $F_{net}=24\ \mathrm{N\,right}$, $a=3\ \mathrm{\frac{m}{s^2}\,right}$, $v=12\ \mathrm{\frac{m}{s}\,right}$

</v-clicks>
