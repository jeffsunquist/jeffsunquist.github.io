## Practice: From Net Force to Motion

An $8\ \mathrm{kg}$ cart is pushed to the right with $40\ \mathrm{N}$ while friction pulls left with $16\ \mathrm{N}$. Starting from rest, the cart moves for $4\ \mathrm{s}$. Find its final velocity.

<div class="absolute bottom-8 left-0 right-0 pr-8 text-right">

<v-clicks>

**Answer:** $v=12\ \mathrm{\frac{m}{s}\,right}$

</v-clicks>
