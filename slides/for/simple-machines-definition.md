## Simple Machines

> [!NOTE]
> A **simple machine** is a device that changes the **size** or **direction** of a force.

((simple_machine_lever: a lever with a load on one side and an effort on the other, showing the smaller effort arrow))

- Machines make work easier by changing the size or direction of a force.
- A machine does **not** reduce the total work done.
