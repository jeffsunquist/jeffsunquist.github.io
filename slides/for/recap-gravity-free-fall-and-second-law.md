## Review: Gravity, Free Fall, and Newton's Second Law

- **Mass** is the amount of matter ($\mathrm{kg}$).
- **Weight** is the force of gravity, $F_g = mg$.
- In **free fall** only gravity acts and the object accelerates at $g$, regardless of mass.
- At **terminal velocity** air resistance balances the weight, the net force is zero, and the velocity is constant.
- **Newton's second law:** $F_{net} = ma$
