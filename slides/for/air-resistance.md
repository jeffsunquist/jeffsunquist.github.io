## Air Resistance

> [!NOTE]
> **Air resistance (drag)** is friction with the air. It opposes motion through the air and **increases** as speed increases.

((parachute_drag: a falling skydiver with and without a parachute, with drag arrows of very different lengths))

- A larger area catches more air, so a parachute creates much more drag.
- Air resistance is why a feather falls more slowly than a coin.
