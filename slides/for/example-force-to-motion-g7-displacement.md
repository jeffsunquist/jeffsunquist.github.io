## Example: Displacement from Acceleration

The cart from the previous slide has $\overset{v_0}{\underline{2\ \mathrm{\frac{m}{s}}}}$ and $\overset{a}{\underline{3\ \mathrm{\frac{m}{s^2}}}}$ to the right. It moves for $\overset{t}{\underline{5\ \mathrm{s}}}$.

Find its displacement.

<v-clicks>

$$\Delta x = v_0 t + \frac{1}{2}at^2 = \left(2\ \mathrm{\frac{m}{s}}\right)(5\ \mathrm{s}) + \frac{1}{2}\left(3\ \mathrm{\frac{m}{s^2}}\right)(5\ \mathrm{s})^2$$

$$\Delta x = 10\ \mathrm{m} + 37.5\ \mathrm{m} = \boxed{47.5\ \mathrm{m\,right}}$$

</v-clicks>
