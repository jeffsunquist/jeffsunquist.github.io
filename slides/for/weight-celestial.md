## Weight on Other Worlds

- On another planet or moon, $g$ is different, so the **weight** of the same object is different.

((celestial_g_comparison: Earth, Moon, and Mars side by side with their g values and the weight of a 1 kg mass))

| World | $g\ (\mathrm{m/s^2})$ | Weight of $1\ \mathrm{kg}$ |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Earth | $9.8$ | $9.8\ \mathrm{N}$ |
| Moon | $1.6$ | $1.6\ \mathrm{N}$ |
| Mars | $3.7$ | $3.7\ \mathrm{N}$ |

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Use $W = mg$ with the **local** value of $g$.
