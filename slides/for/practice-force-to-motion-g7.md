## Practice: Find the Final Velocity

A $\overset{m}{\underline{5\ \mathrm{kg}}}$ box is already moving right at $\overset{v_0}{\underline{4\ \mathrm{\frac{m}{s}}}}$. A push of $\overset{F_{push}}{\underline{30\ \mathrm{N}}}$ acts right while friction is $\overset{F_f}{\underline{20\ \mathrm{N}}}$ left. The box travels $\overset{\Delta x}{\underline{21\ \mathrm{m}}}$.

Find its final velocity.

<div class="absolute bottom-8 left-0 right-0 pr-8 text-right">

<v-clicks>

**Answer:** $F_{net}=10\ \mathrm{N\,right}$, $a=2\ \mathrm{\frac{m}{s^2}\,right}$

$v^2 = v_0^2 + 2a\Delta x = 16 + 84 = 100$, so $v = \boxed{10\ \mathrm{\frac{m}{s}\,right}}$

</v-clicks>
