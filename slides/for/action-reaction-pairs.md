## Action–Reaction Pairs

> [!NOTE]
> An **action–reaction pair** is the two forces in a Newton's third law interaction.

| Action | Reaction |
| --- | --- |
| the foot pushes the ground backward | the ground pushes the foot forward |
| the hand pushes the ball forward | the ball pushes the hand backward |
| the Earth pulls the book down | the book pulls the Earth up |

- The two forces act on **two different objects**.
