## Example: Friction Force

A $10\ \mathrm{kg}$ box slides across a floor with $\mu = 0.4$. Find the friction force.

<v-clicks>

**Solution**

A <mark>$10\ \mathrm{kg}$</mark> box slides with <mark>$\mu = 0.4$</mark>. Find the <mark>friction force</mark>.

$N = mg = 10\ \mathrm{kg} \cdot 9.8\ \mathrm{m/s^2} = 98\ \mathrm{N}$

$f = \mu N = 0.4 \cdot 98\ \mathrm{N} = 39.2\ \mathrm{N}$

$f$: $\mathrm{N}$, opposite the motion

</v-clicks>
