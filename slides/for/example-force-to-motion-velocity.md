## Example: From Acceleration to Velocity

The box from the previous slide has an acceleration of $4\ \mathrm{\frac{m}{s^2}}$ right. It starts from rest and moves for $3\ \mathrm{s}$.

Find its final velocity.

Use $a=\Delta v/t$:

$$\Delta v = at = \left(4\ \mathrm{\frac{m}{s^2}}\right)(3\ \mathrm{s}) = 12\ \mathrm{\frac{m}{s}}$$

$$v = v_0 + \Delta v = 0 + 12\ \mathrm{\frac{m}{s}} = \boxed{12\ \mathrm{\frac{m}{s}\,right}}$$
