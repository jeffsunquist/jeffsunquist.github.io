## Example: From Acceleration to Velocity

The box from the previous slide has an acceleration of $\overset{a}{\underline{4\ \mathrm{\frac{m}{s^2}}}}$ right. It starts from $\overset{v_0}{\underline{\text{rest}}}$ and moves for $\overset{t}{\underline{3\ \mathrm{s}}}$. Find its $\overset{v}{\underline{\text{final velocity}}}$.

<v-clicks>

**Solution:**

$a=\frac{v-v_0}{t} \rightarrow v = v_0 + at$

$v = v_0 + at = 0 + \left(4\ \mathrm{\frac{m}{s^2}}\right)(3\ \mathrm{s}) = \boxed{12\ \mathrm{\frac{m}{s}\,right}}$

</v-clicks>
