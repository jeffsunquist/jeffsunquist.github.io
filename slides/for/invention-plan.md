## Design Brief

1. **Problem** — what job must the machine do?
2. **Machine** — which simple machine(s) will you use, and why?
3. **Plan** — draw and label the parts before building.
4. **Build and test** — does it make the job easier?
5. **Improve** — what would you change?

| Check | Question |
| --- | --- |
| force | does it change the size or direction of the force? |
| work | are you doing less work, or just less force over more distance? |
| safety | can it be used without pinching or tipping? |
