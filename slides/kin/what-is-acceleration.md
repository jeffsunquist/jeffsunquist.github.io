## Acceleration

> [!NOTE] Acceleration
> The **rate at which velocity changes**. Acceleration, $a$, is a vector measured in $\frac{\mathrm{m}}{\mathrm{s^2}}$.

$a=\frac{\Delta v}{t}=\frac{v-v_0}{t}$

An object accelerates when it **speeds up**, **slows down**, or **changes direction**
