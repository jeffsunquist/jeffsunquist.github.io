## Position

>[!NOTE] Position
> The location of an object in a **coordinate system** or relative to a **reference point**. Position, $x$, is a vector and its unit is $\mathrm{m}$

A **coordinate system** has:
- **axes** (show positive and negative directions)
- **origin** (the center of the coordinate system)
- **scale** and **units**
