## Example: Velocity

A bird flies $500\ \mathrm{m}$ east in $25\ \mathrm{s}$. What is its velocity?


<v-clicks>

**Solution**

A bird flies <mark>$500\ \mathrm{m}$ east</mark> in <mark>$25\ \mathrm{s}$</mark>. What is its <mark>velocity</mark>?

$v = \dfrac{\Delta x}{t} = \dfrac{500\ \mathrm{m}}{25\ \mathrm{s}} = 20\ \frac{\mathrm{m}}{\mathrm{s}}\ \mathrm{east}$

$v$: $\frac{\mathrm{m}}{\mathrm{s}}$, east

</v-clicks>
