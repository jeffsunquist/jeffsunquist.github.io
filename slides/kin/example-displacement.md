## Example: Displacement

A balloon floats upward at a constant velocity of $2.5\frac{\mathrm{m}}{\mathrm{s}}$ for a time of $20\ \mathrm{s}$. What is its displacement?


<v-clicks>

**Solution**

A balloon floats <mark>upward</mark> at a <mark>constant velocity of $2.5\frac{\mathrm{m}}{\mathrm{s}}$</mark> for a time of <mark>$20\ \mathrm{s}$</mark>. What is its <mark>displacement</mark>?

$\Delta x=vt =2.5\frac{\mathrm{m}}{\mathrm{s}}\cdot20\ \mathrm{s}=50\ \mathrm{m\ up}$

$\Delta x$: $\mathrm{m}$, up
