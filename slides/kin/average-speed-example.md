## Practice: Average Speed

A van travels <mark>$120\,\mathrm{m}$</mark> east in <mark>$10\,\mathrm{s}$</mark>, then <mark>$80\,\mathrm{m}$</mark> east in <mark>$5\,\mathrm{s}$</mark>, then <mark>$100\,\mathrm{m}$</mark> west in <mark>$5\,\mathrm{s}$ </mark>. What is its <mark>average speed</mark>?

<v-clicks>

- $d_{total} = 120\ \mathrm{m} + 80\ \mathrm{m} + 100\ \mathrm{m} = 300\ \mathrm{m}$
- $t_{total} = 10\ \mathrm{s} + 5\ \mathrm{s} + 5\ \mathrm{s} = 20\ \mathrm{s}$
- $s_{avg}= \frac{d_{\text{total}}}{t_{\text{total}}} = \frac{300\ \mathrm{m}}{20\ \mathrm{s}} = 15\ \frac{\mathrm{m}}{\mathrm{s}}$
- $s_{avg}$: $\frac{\mathrm{m}}{\mathrm{s}}$, scalar

</v-clicks>
