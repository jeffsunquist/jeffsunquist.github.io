## Time

> [!NOTE] Time
The duration between two instants. An instant is a single point on a timeline. Time, $t$, is a scalar measured in $\mathrm{s}$

<v-clicks>

- _A sprinter ran from the starting blocks to the finish line in $10.5\,\mathrm{s}$_.

- _A car braked from $25\,\frac{\mathrm{m}}{\mathrm{s}}$ to $0\,\frac{\mathrm{m}}{\mathrm{s}}$  over $4\,\mathrm{s}$_.

</v-clicks>
