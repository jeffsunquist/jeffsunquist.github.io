## Practice: Average Velocity

A van travels <mark>$120\,\mathrm{m}$ east</mark> in <mark>$10\,\mathrm{s}$</mark>, then <mark>$80\,\mathrm{m}$ east</mark> in <mark>$5\,\mathrm{s}$</mark>, then <mark>$100\,\mathrm{m}$ west</mark> in <mark>$5\,\mathrm{s}$ </mark>. What is its <mark>average velocity</mark>?

<v-clicks>

- $\Delta x_{total} = 120\ \mathrm{m}+80\ \mathrm{m}-100\ \mathrm{m} = 100\ \mathrm{m\ east}$
- $t_{total} = 20\ \mathrm{s}$
- $v_{average}= \frac{\Delta x_{\text{total}}}{t_{\text{total}}} = \frac{100\ \mathrm{m}}{20\ \mathrm{s}} = 5\ \frac{\mathrm{m}}{\mathrm{s}}\ \text{east}$
- $v$: $\frac{\mathrm{m}}{\mathrm{s}}$, $\mathrm{east}$

</v-clicks>
