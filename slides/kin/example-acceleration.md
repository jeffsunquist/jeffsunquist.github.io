## Example: Acceleration

A car speeds up from $5\,\frac{\mathrm{m}}{\mathrm{s}}$ to $25\,\frac{\mathrm{m}}{\mathrm{s}}$ in $4\,\mathrm{s}$. What is its acceleration?

<v-clicks>

**Solution**

A car speeds up from <mark>$5\,\frac{\mathrm{m}}{\mathrm{s}}$</mark> to <mark>$25\,\frac{\mathrm{m}}{\mathrm{s}}$</mark> in <mark>$4\,\mathrm{s}$</mark>. What is its <mark>acceleration</mark>?

$a = \frac{\Delta v}{t} = \frac{v - v_0}{t} = \frac{25 - 5}{4} = 5\,\frac{\mathrm{m}}{\mathrm{s^2}}$

$a$: $\frac{\mathrm{m}}{\mathrm{s^2}}$, positive

</v-clicks>
