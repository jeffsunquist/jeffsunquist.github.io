## Constant Speed and Constant Velocity

> [!NOTE] Constant Speed and Constant Velocity
> Speed is constant when the rate of distance traveled never changes. Velocity is constant when both its magnitude and direction never change.

- Constant at every instant $t$.
- A straight line (constant slope) on a position-time graph.
- Velocity is constant only when moving in a straight line at constant speed.
