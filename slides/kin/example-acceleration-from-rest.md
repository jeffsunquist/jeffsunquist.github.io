## Example: Acceleration from Rest

A skateboard starts from rest and reaches $6\,\frac{\mathrm{m}}{\mathrm{s}}$ in $3\,\mathrm{s}$. What is its acceleration?

<v-clicks>

**Solution**

A skateboard starts from <mark>rest</mark> and reaches <mark>$6\,\frac{\mathrm{m}}{\mathrm{s}}$</mark> in <mark>$3\,\mathrm{s}$</mark>. What is its <mark>acceleration</mark>?

$v_0 = 0$, so $a = \frac{v - 0}{t} = \frac{6}{3} = 2\,\frac{\mathrm{m}}{\mathrm{s^2}}$

$a$: $\frac{\mathrm{m}}{\mathrm{s^2}}$, positive

</v-clicks>
