## Review: Velocity-Time Graphs

- The vertical axis tells you the **velocity** at a given time; the **slope** gives the **acceleration**.
- The **area** under the graph gives the **displacement**: above the axis is positive, below is negative.
- Units of area: $\frac{\mathrm{m}}{\mathrm{s}} \cdot \mathrm{s} = \mathrm{m}$.
- Compare
  - _position-time_ graphs: slope = velocity
  - _velocity-time_ graphs: slope = acceleration
