## Review: Position-Time Graphs

- A **position-time** graph shows position, $x$, on the vertical axis and time, $t$, on the horizontal axis.
- The **slope** of the line is the **velocity**.
- A **flat** line means the object is **at rest**.
- A **steeper** slope means a **greater velocity**.
- A change in direction is a change in the sign of the slope.
