## Example: Distance

A dog runs at a constant speed of $5 \frac{m}{s}$ for a time of $30\ \mathrm{s}$. What is the distance traveled?

<Transform :scale="0.92">
<v-clicks>

**Solution**

A dog runs at a constant speed of <mark>$5 \frac{m}{s}$</mark> for a time of <mark>$30\ \mathrm{s}$</mark>. What is the <mark>distance</mark> traveled?

$d = st = 5 \frac{m}{s} \cdot 30\ \mathrm{s} = 150\ \mathrm{m}$ 

$d$: $\mathrm{m}$, scalar
