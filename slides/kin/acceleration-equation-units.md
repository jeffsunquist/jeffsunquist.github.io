## Acceleration Equation and Units

> [!TIP]
> $a = \frac{\Delta v}{t} = \frac{v - v_0}{t}$
> - $v_0$ = initial velocity, $v$ = final velocity, $t$ = time; $\Delta v = v - v_0$ is the **change in velocity**.
> - Units: $[a] = \dfrac{\mathrm{m}}{\mathrm{s}^2}$ — read "meters per second squared."
