## Relative Velocity (Opposite Directions)

Ship A at $10 \ \frac{\mathrm{m}}{\mathrm{s}}$ east and ship B at $15 \ \frac{\mathrm{m}}{\mathrm{s}}$ west.



<v-clicks>

Assign east as positive and west as negative:
- $v_A = +10 \ \frac{\mathrm{m}}{\mathrm{s}}, \quad v_B = -15 \ \frac{\mathrm{m}}{\mathrm{s}}$

Velocity of B relative to A:
- $v_{BA} = v_B - v_A = -15 \frac{\mathrm{m}}{\mathrm{s}} - 10\frac{\mathrm{m}}{\mathrm{s}} = -25\frac{\mathrm{m}}{\mathrm{s}}=25 \frac{\mathrm{m}}{\mathrm{s}}$ west.

Velocity of A relative to B:
- $v_{AB} = v_A - v_B = 10 \ \frac{\mathrm{m}}{\mathrm{s}} - (-15) \ \frac{\mathrm{m}}{\mathrm{s}} = +25 \frac{\mathrm{m}}{\mathrm{s}}=25 \frac{\mathrm{m}}{\mathrm{s}}$ east.

</v-clicks>
