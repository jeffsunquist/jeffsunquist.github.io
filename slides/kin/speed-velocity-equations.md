## Velocity

> [!NOTE] Velocity
> How fast something moves _and in what direction_. Velocity, $v$, is a vector $\frac{\mathrm{m}}{\mathrm{s}}$.

- $v = \dfrac{\Delta x}{t}$.

<v-click>

- _The velocity of the bus is $40\frac{\mathrm{m}}{\mathrm{s}}\ \mathrm{east}$_.

</v-click>
