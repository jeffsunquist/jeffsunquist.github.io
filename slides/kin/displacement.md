## Displacement

> [!NOTE] Displacement
> A change in position. Displacement, $\Delta x$, is a **vector** and its unit is $\mathrm{m}$.

- $\Delta x = x - x_0$, where $x$ is the _final position_ and $x_0$ is the _initial position_.

- $\Delta x_{total}=\Delta x_1+\Delta x_2+\dots+\Delta x_n$
