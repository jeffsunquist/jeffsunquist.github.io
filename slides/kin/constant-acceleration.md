## Constant Acceleration

- **Constant acceleration**: the velocity changes by the **same amount each second**.
- The acceleration stays the same while the velocity keeps changing.

<v-click>

- Near Earth's surface, free fall is constant acceleration:
  $a = g \approx 9.8\,\frac{\mathrm{m}}{\mathrm{s^2}}$ **downward**.

</v-click>

<v-click>

- A falling object gains about $9.8\,\frac{\mathrm{m}}{\mathrm{s}}$ of speed every second.

</v-click>
