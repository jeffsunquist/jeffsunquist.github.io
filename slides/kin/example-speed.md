## Example: Speed

A train travels $2000\ \mathrm{m}$ in $60\ \mathrm{s}$. What is its speed?



<v-clicks>

**Solution**

A train travels <mark>$2000\ \mathrm{m}$</mark> in <mark>$60\ \mathrm{s}$</mark>. What is its <mark>speed</mark>?

$s = \dfrac{d}{t} = \dfrac{2000\ \mathrm{m}}{60\ \mathrm{s}} = 33.3\ \frac{\mathrm{m}}{\mathrm{s}}$

$s$: $\frac{m}{s}$, scalar

</v-clicks>
