## The Six Simple Machines

| Machine | Description |
| --- | --- |
| lever | a bar that turns about a pivot (fulcrum) |
| wheel and axle | a wheel turning on a smaller axle |
| pulley | a wheel with a rope that changes the direction of a force |
| wedge | a moving inclined surface that splits or separates |
| screw | an inclined plane wrapped around a cylinder |
| inclined plane | a sloped surface that reduces the force needed |
